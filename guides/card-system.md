# Card System & Runes

Welcome to **Card Captor Ragnarok Online**! One of the most distinctive and game-changing features of this server is how cards and equipment customization work. If you are new to the server, understanding this system is crucial for your progression.

## The Card Album System

Unlike traditional Ragnarok Online servers where cards are physical items slotted into equipment, **Card Captor RO introduces an Account-Wide Card Album**.

* **Automatic Collection:** Whenever you obtain a card drop, it is automatically added to your account's Card Album.
* **Account-Wide Effects:** Once a card is in your album, its effect applies **permanently and without restrictions to all characters in your account**.
* **Seasonal Reset:** Please note that the Card Album **resets completely every 3 months** along with the server season, giving everyone a fresh start.

## Transitioning to Runes: How to Gear Up?

Since cards are collected into an account-wide album rather than being slotted into gear, you might wonder: *How do I customize and power up my equipment?*

In this server, equipment customization relies heavily on **Runes** instead of traditional cards. Runes fill the role of customized stat boosts, special effects, and synergies.
